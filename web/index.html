        Library

        Open Bus shows the games in these folders. Nothing is copied or moved, and saves go beside each game.

          Your games stay in this browser. Add them, or drop files or whole folders anywhere on the page: covers and game lists beside them come too.

            Games

            Everything bigger, for playing on a TV from across the room: the library and its games fullscreen.

            In a game, F11 switches between the window and fullscreen.

            A game closed is saved as it was, and goes on from there next time. F12 resets it, for a fresh start.

            Hold Backspace, or a gamepad's left trigger, to go back through a NES game's last 30 seconds.

            A red light at the corner of the picture while a disk drive is busy, as on the drive itself.

            For slow computers: the NES draws a line at a time. Nearly all games look the same, but a few effects won't be exact.

            What the NES's RAM holds when it's switched on. A real console's is whatever its chip comes up with; a few games depend on it.

            Controls

            Keyboard and gamepad, for both players. In a game, tap Select and Start together, or press F1, for a menu with this screen in it. To get back here from a game, hold Select and Start, or press F5.In a game, its Controls button has this screen too.
